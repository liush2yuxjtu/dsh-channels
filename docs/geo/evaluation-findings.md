# GEO measurement findings

Observed on 2026-11-10. The bounded source diagnostic rose from 7/8 to 8/8 after installation prerequisites and the executable command were placed together. The facts already existed in the baseline; the gap was passage locality, not a missing product capability.

The actual DeepSeek source-grounded judge passed all ten selected questions in each of two baseline and two final samples. It therefore measured **no model-answer improvement** from the documentation change. Two deterministic source-order permutations are a small robustness check, not a representative population estimate.

Manual review found an important blind spot: the Slack non-fit case correctly rejected DSH Channels but left `recommendedProducts` empty, even while naming Devin and Claude Tag in its explanation. The v1 pass rule tested target fit and URL membership; it did not test competitor recall or citation entailment. A perfect v1 score must not be described as perfect product selection. Our full documentation also occupied more input than each competitor research summary. A separate balanced-card selection benchmark addresses the recall and input-budget problems; it does not retroactively change the frozen v1 scores.

Exa returned no target URL among the first eight results for eight training queries and four held-out queries. These are twelve single-sample observations before publication of this candidate, not twelve public AI answers. They do not establish Google indexing status, a ranking cause, or future visibility. Generated-answer citation and recommendation rates remain unmeasured.

The three content mechanisms reached a local diagnostic plateau. The workflow and limits additions were retained for readers, with zero measured gain. This finite stop is not proof that no more useful optimization exists. The next external experiment needs newly captured search/assistant observations after discovery systems have had an opportunity to read published sources; repeating a saturated keyword diagnostic cannot supply that evidence.

The corrected capped-card selection test passed six cases in each of two actual provider responses. It routed Slack tasks to Slack products and standing-team tasks to agent-team products instead of DSH Channels, and abstained on unsupported security guarantees. These 12/12 curated checks establish correct routing in this supplied corpus; they establish no public discovery improvement.
